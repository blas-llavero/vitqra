# Scientific and clinical validation center — v0.6.3 initial

A closed, memory-only Unity editor demonstration of independent-review workflow. No actual identity, credentials, authentication, scientific evaluation, certificate, signature, external submission, patient records, data imports, persistence, network or clinical decision. All inputs are fictional. The existing v0.5.6a unsigned package request is unchanged; this does not import or approve actual research packages.

## Workflow and quality gate

One DossierA fixture starts with the v0.6.2a DifficultDaysOnly quality report (0 valid scheduled slots of 30). A favorable draft is blocked until the numerical fixture gate is ReadyForReview. An assigned fictional reviewer may request corrections or reject. The editor-only coordinator can explicitly replace a revision-requested fixture with a new Regular quality fixture (30/30), retaining the old revision and decisions. Maximum three revisions; rejected and favorable-simulation revisions are closed. This is not a real data correction or an author permission service; the replacement fixture is separately invented and not a repair of a person's data. ReadyForReview is not evidence of representativeness or research authorization.

Each revision hash binds protocol, resource, revision number, exact quality-report digest and fixed fictional method identifier. A decision binds that hash and revision, fixture day, reviewer, verdict and enumerated rationale. Prior decisions never carry forward. Domain and Methods are two distinct assigned fictional identities; both must submit favorable drafts on the current revision for FavorableSimulation. Conflicted and Unverified fixtures deny. Duplicate decisions, stale hashes, invalid rationale combinations, closed revisions and expired access deny. Fixed v0.6.0 Reviewer role, DossierA, IndependentReview Read/SubmitReviewDraft grants required at access time. No approval action is added to that policy. The user-selectable editor identities are not authentication; no real independence or qualifications verified. A single unfavorable draft closes the current fixture; this is a provisional governance design, not a legal rule or a real panel process.

Actual scientific, clinical, regulatory approval, certification and clinical release are always false, even after two favorable simulated drafts. Hashes are not signatures. No real clinical or regulatory authority is simulated as granting permission. Histories in memory are not durable, tamper-evident audit logs. No patient or reviewer personal data published.
