# Data quality, completeness and informative sampling — v0.6.2a

Author requirement: do not treat fragmented records or observations concentrated on difficult days as representative evidence. Record stable days and difficult days on a clinically justified schedule, distinguish an explicit absence of symptoms from a missing observation, and assess completeness, representativeness and observation-process bias BEFORE research/model fitting or independent review. Applies to future patient contributions and imported research datasets, not only images. This milestone is a closed synthetic demonstrator, not a real data intake gate or clinical service.

## Implemented closed protocol

One prespecified 30-day fictional daily schedule for one unitless 0–10 feature; an explicit zero is a valid measurement. Scheduled and event-triggered entries are distinct; extra entries never fill scheduled missing slots. Missing entries, explicit nulls, invalid values, duplicate scheduled entries and delayed availability are not imputed. Only observations and availability at or before the information cutoff are visible. The denominator is scheduled slots due, not the number of submissions. Report observed, missing, extras, longest gap, recorded low/high counts (fictional threshold 7), invalid/duplicate counts, scheduled-only descriptive mean and a heuristic observation-pattern warning. No forced 50/50 class balance: true prevalence and a stable symptom-free trajectory may be legitimate. No claim to infer MNAR from available records. The warning prompts review rather than rejecting stable people. SHA-256 covers the visible representation only, not an approval or trusted signature.

Default strict fixture gate: invalid data => InvalidData; window not finished => AwaitingWindow; fewer than all 30 valid scheduled measurements => InsufficientCoverage; otherwise ReadyForReview. A delayed measurement may enter at a later cutoff, not retroactively. ReadyForReview is NOT permission to start research, proof of absence of bias, clinical validity, or certification: CanUseForResearch and ClinicalRelease are always false. The 100% threshold and daily cadence are provisional synthetic protocol choices, not universal medical requirements. A universal complete-case rule can itself introduce selection bias and exclude severely ill patients. Real protocols must set justified completeness rules prospectively and permit an independently reviewed missing-data plan when scientifically appropriate.

Nine fictional scenarios: regular, difficult-day-only extras, contiguous gap, late arrival, duplicate, missing value, stable zero-only trajectory, regular with extra events, invalid value. JavaScript reference reproduction: 9 cases, 279 scenario/cutoff invariants, 4 additional boundaries. The C# editor test is supplied but not compiled or run. No actual dataset or production pipeline is changed. No personal information, network, new dependencies, patient-facing alerts or automatic research. Use editor menu VITQRA > Run Synthetic Data Quality Checks.

## Future required gate for authorized datasets

- Prespecify population, clinically meaningful time windows, baseline/pre-event observation schedule, required variables/modalities and outcome/follow-up ascertainment. Join fragmented sources only through authorized linkage, harmonize units/timestamps, validate provenance, duplicates, conflicts, instrument changes and subject identity. No fabrication or silently equating no record with no disease. Record reason for missingness where feasible; no forced responses or extra photos.
- Report coverage by participant, time interval, modality, site, population subgroup and outcome group; examine gaps, irregular sampling, scheduled vs event-triggered contact, dropout and selection effects. Evaluate representation against intended population rather than forcing equal numbers of good and bad days, events or classes. Preserve real prevalence in evaluation; any training reweighting must be documented and confined to training.
- Use feasible clinician/researcher-designed schedules, optional nonintrusive reminders, accessible forms and explicit 'no symptom', 'unknown', 'not applicable', 'not measured' and 'decline' states. Do not postpone care or penalize people for missing entries. More data is not always appropriate.
- Prespecify treatment of missing data, distinguish assumptions (MCAR/MAR/MNAR), investigate informative observation, sensitivity analyses, and appropriate statistical methods (e.g. multiple imputation where justified, observation-process weighting/joint models where assumptions support them). Never invent historical observations or treat estimates as measured data. Fit preprocessing and imputation only within development data; avoid future/outcome leakage. Compare complete-case and alternative analyses and report uncertainty, excluded participants and reasons.
- Require a versioned dataset-quality report bound to provenance, authorized purpose, exact dataset and protocol; independent methodologist review before any sensitive-data/model pipeline may run. Insufficient data blocks automatic use for the affected task, not healthcare. Retain existing scientific, privacy and clinical release gates. External valid datasets need not be 100% complete if a prespecified, independently justified protocol addresses limitations. No real data until governance and backend are implemented.
