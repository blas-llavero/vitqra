# Community and resource authorization — v0.6.0 initial

Closed synthetic policy prototype only. No actual authentication, role verification, patient consent, clinical records, access tokens, server, network, database, chat, recordings or live collaboration. Never use caller-selectable enums or the editor preview as production identity or authorization. Decisions cannot release a product or approve a result. Existing research/XR UI and release gates unchanged.

## Policy

Deny by default; require independently verified identity in a future server, role ceiling, exact resource, intended purpose, action, valid time and nonrevoked grant. Fixture days are integers 0–180, not real time; start inclusive, end exclusive, revocation effective from its day. No administrator or AI bypass. The fixed nonoverlapping grants cannot be edited by the preview. Patient fixtures can read/add own observations; clinicians read their assigned records during the grant; researchers read a specific study and aggregate; industry reads only a specific aggregate; reviewers read a specific dossier and submit an unsigned draft. Community participation and private room entry are separate grants; moderators cannot access clinical records. Community B, Study B, Aggregate B and Dossier B intentionally lack grants. No export, recording, approval or grant creation. Actual aggregate disclosure control, redaction and reidentification protection remain pending. The education resource is symbolic. No resource payload is present. Decision summaries are fictional deterministic strings, not a persistent or tamper-evident audit.

## Future deployment design

- Separate clinical vault, research workspace, independent review workspace, peer support, consultation rooms and reviewed education. Separate storage/keys and minimal identifiers; room membership never grants access to records. Separate membership, role verification and resource grants.
- Choose and review an open-source identity provider and server-side policy enforcement before deployment. Verify institutions, clinicians and reviewers; use least privilege, MFA where appropriate, session expiry, issuer/audience checks, secure token storage and revocation. Never trust roles, resource IDs, expiry or time supplied by the client. Bind permissions to authenticated subject, institution, resource version, purpose and operation; enforce on every request and object including media and streaming channels; recheck active subscriptions after revocation. No reliance on a hidden button or headset-local decision.
- Clinical and research consent and lawful basis are distinct; institutional approval, data agreements, ethics and purpose limitation require separate governance. Define emergency access separately rather than invent a bypass. Enforce withdrawal, access revocation, retention, safe audit, incident handling and data protection assessment. Community identity is optional and pseudonymous to other participants; no inferred or disclosed condition. Moderation, safeguarding and reporting required.
- Industry receives reviewed disclosure-controlled aggregates by default, with appropriate group size and privacy review; no patient recruitment or direct records. Independent reviewers need conflict checks, authority and signature verification. Neither an unsigned draft nor a role grants formal approval.
- No patient data in ordinary research reports, GitHub or consumer AI connectors. No recording by default. Authorization does not establish clinical or regulatory approval. No real data or deployment until independent security, privacy, governance and applicable regulatory review.
