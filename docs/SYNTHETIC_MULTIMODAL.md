# Synthetic multimodal research — v0.5.9

Closed experiment only. No disease-specific protocol, actual image pixels, genomic sequences, symptoms, biomarker units, real patient records, data access, network, clinical prediction or clinical use. The four modality names identify synthetic scalar proxies, not validated feature extractors. Separate from the v0.5.8 fixture.

Protocol synthetic-multimodal-v1: fixed seed 271828183, 600 fictional subjects, cutoff day 60, binary fictional event by day 120. Four independent latent integers and noise generate unitless temporal change, image, symptom and genetic proxies. Event probability is deliberately constructed from the four latent variables. Some measurements are missing or delayed, follow-up may stop at day 90. Each modality also has a deliberately leaky future observation, excluded by both observed and available dates. Unknown outcomes are excluded before incomplete-feature cases. No imputation. All 600 subjects undergo future/delayed value perturbation checks. No individual score is returned to users.

Fixed subject-level split: ID modulo three equal to zero is holdout (200 assigned), other IDs development (400). All five models use the same complete-case participants. Report availability for each modality among known-outcome subjects separately; missingness can introduce selection bias. Unimodal scores are individual proxy values (temporal is last minus first); fusion is their prespecified unweighted sum. Each threshold is fit on development only, by maximum balanced accuracy over integer thresholds -33 through 33; ties choose higher threshold. Holdout is fixed and public, not a fresh independent clinical cohort. Compare with always-negative baseline. Report confusion counts, balanced accuracy and nominal Wilson 95% intervals separately for sensitivity and specificity. These are conditional sampling intervals, not calibrated individual risk estimates or simultaneous inference. No formal paired superiority test, external validation, censoring adjustment, subgroup analysis or clinical benefit claim. Do not optimize using these holdout results.

Independent JavaScript reproduction during source preparation: 188 development and 88 holdout eligible. Holdout unknown=12, incomplete=100. Results in verification/multimodal-v059/results.json. The supplied C# checks assert the reference counts and thresholds; Unity compilation and execution are pending. The digest binds the full synthetic fixture including later outcomes; it is NOT an input feature, identity proof or signature. Editor-only preview: VITQRA > Synthetic Multimodal Preview. Menu labels are English development tools; preview labels follow device language in ten languages. No runtime XR changes.

Real multimodal research needs a disease-specific prespecified endpoint, suitable authorized data, reviewed extraction and preprocessing, locked versions, leakage control, participant/site/time splits, external validation, calibration, missingness and subgroup analysis, uncertainty, clinical utility and independent scientific/clinical governance. No automatic medical alert, diagnosis, treatment or release. Future community work must maintain isolated data stores and resource-level permissions.
