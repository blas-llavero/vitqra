# Scientific and clinical validation workflow — v0.5.6a

Author requirement: manage the complete evidence review and formal approval workflow through VITQRA rather than treating AI outputs as validated. This milestone adds an unsigned review-request.txt to newly generated research archives. It binds exact UTF-8 TXT, HTML and reproducibility record digests; the enclosing archive manifest also covers the PDF and request bytes. The package identity changes; prior files are preserved. The request is deterministic, size-bounded and recomputed on verification. It has fixed pending states and a blocked clinical release. It contains no actual reviewer identity, signature, certificate or authorization. It is not a clinical evaluation report, CE marking, peer-review certificate, or full independent reproduction bundle. Hashes are not signatures or proof of authenticity. No external submission, review backend, clinical data intake or clinical decision is implemented.

## Target workflow

1. Define intended purpose, target population, use environment, version, comparator, outcome, prediction horizon and predefined acceptance thresholds. Distinguish research-only hypotheses from intended medical use. Determine applicable jurisdiction and product classification with qualified regulatory expertise; an app workflow alone cannot determine legal conformity.
2. Compile evidence in a controlled workspace: licensed datasets, study protocol, reference standard, data provenance, participant/site/time separation, code/dependency versions, model weights/version, seeds, uncertainty, deviations, failed tests and reproducibility. Record literature searches and critical appraisal; do not treat an AI-generated citation or multiple AI votes as evidence.
3. Evaluate the association between outputs and intended condition, technical/analytical performance, and clinical performance as distinct evidence components (MDCG 2020-1). Use appropriate external datasets and where necessary prospective studies. For longitudinal predictions, exclude future data and use participant-level separation; measure calibration, sensitivity, specificity, predictive values, false alert burden and clinical utility against a meaningful baseline. No universal pass score. For therapeutic research, separate computational findings, preclinical evidence, clinical trials and medicinal-product authorization; software conformity is not authorization of a drug.
4. Independent expert review in a secure 2D or XR workspace. Verify individual identity, qualifications and institution, role-specific authorization, conflicts of interest and independence. Proposed default: at least two independent reviewers for scientific assessment (domain and methods expertise), plus relevant clinical and regulatory specialists when applicable. This is a project governance proposal, not a universal legal requirement. Record accept, reject or request revision with rationale and limitations for an exact dossier revision; no AI can approve or impersonate a reviewer. Industry funding or room membership grants no review authority.
5. Formal approval workflow: separate signed expert findings, institutional/ethics decisions, regulatory documents and release decision. Use independently verified issuer credentials and a reviewed signature implementation, trusted timestamps, signature validation and revocation, authority scope and audit trail; assess eIDAS requirements when applicable. Preserve originals in a private controlled store; never publish signatures, reviewer personal details or patient information to GitHub. A signed expert review is not a regulatory certificate. VITQRA may display verified externally issued certificates and their exact scope, validity and status, but cannot issue them on behalf of an authority. A future regulatory interface must not submit anything without explicit authorized action.
6. For medical device software under MDR, plan and document clinical evaluation and follow the applicable conformity assessment procedure. Where required, involve a notified body; the manufacturer is responsible for the applicable EU declaration of conformity and CE marking. For clinical investigations in Spain under Article 62 with non-CE devices, follow applicable AEMPS authorization, favorable CEIm opinion and site agreement requirements before starting. Assess IVDR, AI Act, GDPR and Spanish law where relevant. Requirements depend on intended purpose and classification; this document is a design reference, not a legal determination. Clinical validation of an output, ethics permission to study it and permission to market/use a product are distinct.
7. Release only within the independently approved intended purpose, version, population and limitations, after all applicable gates are satisfied and a separate accountable release decision. Track post-market surveillance, incidents, performance drift, withdrawals, expiry and reassessment. A new model, dataset, preprocessing or clinically significant change triggers impact assessment and applicable revalidation before release. Automatic model catalog updates must not change a clinically approved model without controlled review. Never silently substitute a consumer AI model for a validated model.

## Architecture and implementation sequence

- v0.5.6a: local unsigned dossier and fixed pending status (this source milestone).
- v0.5.7–v0.5.9: synthetic longitudinal and multimodal research, preregistered protocols and independent evaluation; no clinical release.
- v0.6.x: authenticated role-based collaboration, independent reviewers, revision queues, conflicts, secure evidence store and auditable decisions. Patient/community workspaces remain isolated.
- Later regulated workflow: institutional integration, verified signatures and externally issued approval documents, independent governance, clinical studies as required, quality management and conformity assessment. No real patient data until privacy, security, ethics and legal requirements are met. No clinical use solely because a version number is reached.

Keep the approved scientific/clinical model pinned independently of the research-mode automatic model selector. Current educational providers and ordinary report exports must never receive patient data or restricted clinical records. Do not add new paid APIs or dependencies without the existing review. No automatic external submission.
