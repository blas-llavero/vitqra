# Synthetic clinical previsit workspace — v0.6.1 initial

Closed editor demonstration only. Two fictional patient record resources, assigned fictional patients and clinicians, one hypothetical visit at fixture day 30. No real patients, medical measurements, symptoms, images, image capture, consent, EHR import, upload, storage, networking, AI inference, diagnosis, medical advice, alert or clinical approval.

The policy reuses the v0.6.0 exact resource grants, with Care/Read fixed. The access day and historical information cutoff are separate: the access day must have a current grant; the cutoff cannot be in the future or on/after the scheduled visit. Revoked/expired access cannot be recovered by selecting an earlier cutoff. A denied call returns no summary or digest. The editor recomputes on every draw and retains no cached summary after a denied request. This is not production authentication or a security boundary; fixture identity/time are user-selectable, not authoritative. Previously viewed information cannot be retroactively erased by access denial.

Each synthetic record has a deterministic set of 10 observations. Numeric A and B are arbitrary unitless values; symptom marker is a fictitious number, image and visit question are placeholders without actual content. Each row has ID, observation and availability days, type, provenance, status and optional numeric value. Only visible observations enter the summary; future, late-unavailable and visit-day rows do not contribute to counts or digest. Missing values remain missing; placeholders are not measurements. First-to-last numeric differences require two comparable measured values. No inferred symptom severity, risk score, interpretation, treatment suggestion or urgency ranking. SHA-256 binds the exact resource, cutoff, visit day and visible data, not reviewer identity or authorization. No signature or approval. No export to ordinary research packages or AI. Future clinical storage must be isolated and authenticated.

Editor: VITQRA > Synthetic Previsit Preview. Choose fictional identity/record, current access day and historical cutoff; view the unsigned summary, two unconnected numeric point series, visible rows and digest. Core labels in ten device languages; enum/provenance/status identifiers and development menu remain English. No runtime XR layout modification. The closed fixtures are not a clinical chart or actual patient input.

Verification: JavaScript reproduction checks 134664 access/cutoff combinations (7150 allowed, 127514 denied) and reference observations. C# editor test includes matching enumeration, cross-patient and AI denial, revocation/expiry, future cutoff, resource-bound digest, missingness and closed release/export gates. Unity compilation and actual C# execution are pending. Reference design is shared, not independent security certification.
