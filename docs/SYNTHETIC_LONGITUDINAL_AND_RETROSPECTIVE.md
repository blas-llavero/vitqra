# Longitudinal and retrospective research — initial local prototype

## v0.5.7: closed timeline

Three fictional subjects with two unitless features at seven scheduled times. The immutable observations record observation day, availability day, nullable value and a simulated research-scope flag. Only records both observed and available at the cutoff, for the selected subject and allowed scope, enter the snapshot. Missing values remain missing; differences are calculated from the first and last measured values only. SHA-256 binds the eligible snapshot, not the identity or validity of a source. The fixture flag is not real consent or an access control mechanism. The editor displays points without interpolation. No data import, persistence, networking or runtime XR panel.

## v0.5.8: retrospective synthetic experiment

Fixed deterministic seed 20261126; 120 fictional subjects. Each subject has measurements scheduled at days 0, 30, 60 and 100. Some measurements are missing or delayed. A fictional binary event is generated with a noisy relationship to a latent feature. Event times lie in days 75–120; follow-up ends at day 90 or 120. An event after the end of follow-up is unobserved. The observation at day 100 is deliberately correlated with the generated outcome as a leakage test; it is never eligible for the feature score at day 60.

Protocol: predict a synthetic event by day 120 from measurements observed and available at day 60. Require at least two eligible numeric measurements; use the last minus the first, without imputing missing data. Unknown outcomes (insufficient follow-up without an observed event) are excluded first, then missing-feature cases. The resulting complete-case analysis may be biased; no censoring adjustment or confidence intervals are implemented. Partition by synthetic subject ID modulo three, with 80 assigned to development and 40 to holdout. Choose the threshold from -20 to 21 on the development group alone by maximizing balanced accuracy; ties choose the larger threshold. Evaluate the fixed threshold on the holdout, comparing with an always-negative baseline. Report TP, FP, TN, FN, sensitivity, specificity, positive and negative predictive values, balanced accuracy and denominators. Undefined metrics are null, not zero. These are classification metrics, not calibrated risk probabilities.

## Independent Python reproduction

543 timeline cutoff checks and 120 perturbation checks that replace future/late-arriving feature values with extreme values without changing the score. Subject separation, eligibility accounting, delayed availability, missingness and determinism also asserted. Results: 66 development and 35 holdout cases; threshold 2; holdout TP=7, FP=6, TN=15, FN=7. Holdout balanced accuracy 60.71%; always-negative baseline 50%. The synthetic event is intentionally constructed with an association and the holdout is a fixed public fixture, not independent real-world validation. No clinical value, lead time or disease detection is established. No adjustment of the threshold after inspecting holdout results in this protocol.

## Release and next steps

This is an editor-only demonstration of time-cutoff hygiene and evaluation bookkeeping. It has no disease-specific feature extraction, calibrated probabilities, patient-facing alerts, individual clinical predictions, dataset access, regulatory approval or actual consent/authorization. Real studies require reviewed dataset licenses, suitable cohorts and endpoints, participant/site/time split, a prespecified protocol, independent validation, uncertainty, bias and clinical utility assessment, and separate clinical and privacy governance. Preserve the current scientific and clinical release gates; no consumer AI transfer. Unity C# and physical Quest tests are pending. The initial overlay was published to main at 616f2bc and integrated into development at fb9171a. The next source milestone should be a condition-specific, approved-data protocol and experimental multimodal evaluation, not deployment.
